01 · THE PROBLEM

Detect risk before it becomes visible.

Mastitis can affect milk production, animal welfare and farm economics. MastiTrack focuses on an early-warning workflow.

🔎

Late detection

The project targets forecasting before obvious visible symptoms appear.

🥛

Milk quality & yield

Early identification can help reduce production loss and support timely action.

👨‍🌾

Smallholder focus

The proposed design is intended for smallholder farms and low-cost deployment.

05 · FEASIBILITY

Designed around real-world constraints.

01

Sensor fouling

Milk fat can affect readings and cause fouling.

Response → Regular probe cleaning + durable, food-safe electrode design.
02

Temperature variation

Farm-level temperature changes can distort raw readings.

Response → Built-in temperature compensation concept.
03

Limited labelled data

Healthy vs infected real-world labels are needed for training.

Response → Proposed partnerships with veterinary colleges/dairy cooperatives.
04

Farmer adoption

A new rural technology needs trust and usability.

Response → Simple vernacular interface + demonstrations.
